## Screening model, not a certification

Every 0.5 game seconds (and on pulse admission), bounded midpoint integration of full 3D body-centred inertial position/velocity and rotating surface-relative velocity. Gravity varies as mu/r^2. Test coast for preparation reserve, then retrograde thrust with no credit while ascending, using 80% of min(current fixed-limited and transient-limited available acceleration); constant snapshot capability, no fuel mass-loss or aerodynamic deceleration credit. Current drag-aware ordinary predictor remains in use for targeting; no new aerodynamic model or drag mutation. Protected terrain is global PQS upper radius (also current terrain if higher) plus current bottom offset; require >=300 m remaining clearance and <=15 m/s surface speed, and sufficient ideal hover authority. At most 1200 s, 5000 steps; 1 s midpoint, 0.25 s near capture; chord and midpoint clearance checks. If invalid, bounded horizon, or no certified delay, commit; no deadline from integration start or speed/vertical-height shortcut. Deliberately conservative, may brake unnecessarily early; adverse lift, variable atmosphere-dependent thrust/attitude, terrain envelope and terminal dynamics not certified. No claimed optimal ignition UT. In unknown attitude conditions reserve 5–30 s depending on angle, or 1.5x measured V1 turn/settling if greater; 2x engine response, smoothing, measured warp overrun, 3 s guard, 0.5 s sampling and tick. These are test parameters, not measured worst-case bounds. Tested thrust UT is a candidate, not a latest validated ignition time. On failed delay also record immediate probe feasibility. No attempt to fit to Duna crash summary alone.
