Body-centred inertial r/v, co-rotating airflow, spherical central gravity, pressure/density curves vs ASL, drag opposing air-relative velocity. Curves use explicit linear or supplied Hermite tangents and reject out-of-domain values. This is not yet a complete KSP atmosphere adapter: latitude/solar/temperature, actual drag cubes, passive lift, real terrain/contact and body metadata are still required. No active aero steering.
- Pressure-dependent Isp and per-engine flow with g0, persistent limit and separate torque-balanced available force. Exact restricted allocator for one/two verified collinear fixed-axis engines with opposing moments about fixed CoM; zero net torque maximum, no presumed other actuator torque. Nonopposed moment geometry unsupported, unknown capability rather than unrestricted scalar. No gimbal, arbitrary multi-engine, minimum physical throttle, resource crossfeed/staging, differential allocator fidelity or changing CoM yet. Propulsion requires supplied verified geometry; synthetic inputs are labelled. Pressure changes force and Isp; mass changes on finite burn. Separate first-order engine rise/fall and bounded forward-axis slew; no roll dynamics/full angular acceleration; contact rate is sampled forward-axis rate, not measured full 6DoF.
- Midpoint step, bounded step <=1 s and candidate <=1800 s/100000 iterations, cancellation at candidate steps. Spherical contact and fuel reserve crossing bracketed, chord crossing uncertain; no certified continuous-time or terrain-path validity. ContactWithinLimits only relative to synthetic spherical model, vertical/horizontal/pointing/pointing rate gates. Not safe touchdown claim for actual vessel. FirstPoweredUT reports start of first powered integration interval, including correction; not exact engine ignition or brake-only event.
- Screen explicitly propagates finite correction, tail and coast to tested ignition, turn toward retrograde, then actual bounded direction/throttle with descent/alignment gate. Does NOT reject before integration for a static hover assumption. CaptureEnvelope is low surface speed above specified reserve, not validated terminal landing. Both delayed/immediate failures mean no demonstrated candidate, not necessarily physical impossibility and not a braking deadline. Unknown capability/horizon/cancellation remain unresolved. EarlierCandidateOnly means a tested earlier candidate succeeded and tested delayed failed, not a global latest-safe boundary. Entry cutoff independent; Classify CandidateFits is non-actuating screen result, not clearance to command a correction. Caller must supply appropriate entry cutoff, correction/tail and preparation; no coupling to live correction yet. Preserve effective early correction by screening finite manoeuvre plus future braking and later selecting best feasible landing; do not permanently abandon on single failed probe. Need optimized target timing and terminal controller before M2/M4/M5.
- Model not yet matched to QS226 orbital state/body; no actual per-engine live allocations/torque/gimbal/pressure/force curves, terrain corridor, mass flow or drag/lift parity. Saved engine ledger baseline 100% but applied ~90%; observed available force varied 799–1012 kN then 782.633, cap 1.0; do not freeze trim or assume nominal 1440 kN. Real body/propulsion inputs and independently verified sustained force envelope required before use. Do not relax guard by merely substituting local gravity/removing margin.

### Implemented contracts

- `AtmosphericLandingDynamics.Forces` exposes the same evaluation used by midpoint propagation. State time, inertial position/velocity/attained forward direction, mass, normalized actual engine response, pressure/density and co-rotating air-relative velocity are explicit. Gravity and total acceleration m/s²; thrust, drag and lift N; flow kg/s; pressure Pa, density kg/m³. Force decomposition is not a new control law. Unknown capability or nonfinite force returns an unknown sample, not a substitute zero.
- Optional deterministic atmosphere callback (UT, body-centred inertial position → pressure/density) supports longitude/latitude/time-dependent data; explicit joint aerodynamic callback (UT, position, air-relative velocity, attained forward, pressure, density → drag/lift forces). No KSP objects on worker threads and no callback into mutable game state intended; immutable adapters supplied by caller. Reject unknown or invalid callback data, positive work by passive drag, and ambiguous simultaneous constant-area/legacy-lift plus joint adapter. Vacuum explicitly returns zero aerodynamic force. Original constant-area/Hermite and optional lift paths retained for synthetic tests. Full KSP temperature, Mach and roll/geometry-dependent aerodynamic adapter remains unimplemented; forward axis alone does not determine roll-sensitive lift. Exact scalar altitude pressure lookup retains strict domain; does not extrapolate from known contact.
- `AtmosphericLandingReplay.Run` accepts initial physical state, contiguous time-stamped piecewise-constant throttle/direction commands, optional same-epoch observations, bounded step count and cancellation. Propagate without resetting to observations; split integration at command and observation epochs; reject missing intervals and ambiguous/out-of-order data. Terminal contact, reserve exhaustion, unsupported model, cancellation and horizon stop replay. Finishing a command history is not a touchdown result. No automatic use of a capture envelope as a terminal landing.
- Report independent state errors (position, velocity, mass and pointing), predicted force decomposition and force model evaluated at observed state. If independent measured force and actual normalized engine response are supplied, compare thrust/drag/lift vectors and flow at that observed state; this separates force-model error from accumulated state error. Never substitute pilot/applied throttle for measured engine response or maximum possible flow for actual consumed flow. Missing force observations remain unknown; not inferred from the predicted force. Same-epoch, frame and data lineage must be verified externally. These are error reporting primitives, not established flight tolerance or a fitted model.
- Explicit current limitations: sphere/constant bottom offset, bounded forward-axis kinematics rather than full angular acceleration/roll, restricted one/two verified collinear fixed-engine geometry, no actual KSP actuator allocator, no physical terminal controller or terrain corridor adapter, no independent aero parity. The existing QS226 nominal engine figures and body constants are evidence, not justification to invent lever arms, pressure curves, throttle state, available fuel or drag/lift. Unknown model values cannot authorize a candidate. No new live control, solver selection or M2/M3 work.
